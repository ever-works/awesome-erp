- [SAP S/4HANA](https://www.sap.com/products/erp/s4hana.html) - SAP S/4HANA is SAP's flagship enterprise resource planning suite built on the SAP HANA in-memory database. It targets large enterprises with modules for finance, supply chain, manufacturing, and sales. Licensing is enterprise-grade and sold through SAP sales channels. ([Read more](/details/sap-s-4hana.md)) `Enterprise` `Paid` `API` `AI-Powered` `In-Memory`

## Open-Source ERP

- [Dolibarr](https://www.dolibarr.org/) - Dolibarr is an open-source ERP and CRM suite for small businesses, freelancers, and foundations. It includes invoicing, inventory, and point-of-sale features. It can be self-hosted or used via cloud partners under a free open-source license. ([Read more](/details/dolibarr.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Free Plan` `API` `CRM`
- [ERPNext](https://erpnext.com/) - ERPNext is an open-source ERP system for small and medium businesses covering accounting, inventory, manufacturing, and HR. It is developed by Frappe Technologies and available as a self-hosted community edition or paid cloud subscription. The core software is free and open source. ([Read more](/details/erpnext.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Free Plan` `API` `Accounting` `Inventory`
- [Ever Gauzy](https://gauzy.co/) - Ever Gauzy is an open-source business management suite combining ERP, CRM, and e-commerce features. It targets small and medium businesses seeking a self-hosted platform. The core is released under an open-source license with optional paid cloud services. ([Read more](/details/ever-gauzy.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Free Plan` `API` `E-commerce`
- [iDempiere](https://www.idempiere.org/) - iDempiere is an open-source ERP built on the ADempiere codebase and OSGi framework. It supports accounting, supply chain, and CRM for small to mid-sized organizations. The software is free to download and self-host under an open-source license. ([Read more](/details/idempiere.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Free Plan` `API` `Accounting`

## SMB ERP

- [Acumatica](https://www.acumatica.com/) - Acumatica is a cloud ERP platform for small and mid-sized businesses across distribution, manufacturing, and retail. It provides financials, project accounting, and CRM with unlimited user licensing. Pricing is based on resources consumed rather than per-user seats. ([Read more](/details/acumatica.md)) `Paid` `Cloud` `API` `Manufacturing` `Distribution`
- [Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central](https://dynamics.microsoft.com/business-central/overview) - Dynamics 365 Business Central is a cloud ERP for small and mid-sized businesses. It combines financials, supply chain, sales, and service management with Microsoft 365 integration. It is offered as a paid monthly subscription per user. ([Read more](/details/microsoft-dynamics-365-business-central.md)) `Paid` `API` `Cloud` `Microsoft 365` `Financials`
- [Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/) - Odoo is an open-source ERP suite with modules for CRM, e-commerce, accounting, manufacturing, and inventory. It serves small and mid-sized businesses and offers a free community edition plus paid enterprise cloud services. Pricing is freemium with paid hosting and support. ([Read more](/details/odoo.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Free Plan` `Freemium` `API` `Modular`
- [Sage Intacct](https://www.sage.com/products/sage-intacct) - Sage Intacct is a cloud financial management and ERP solution for small and mid-sized businesses. It focuses on core accounting, multi-entity consolidation, and financial reporting. It is sold as a paid subscription with tiered pricing. ([Read more](/details/sage-intacct.md)) `Paid` `Cloud` `API` `Accounting` `Financials`
